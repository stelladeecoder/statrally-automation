## What is StatRally?

StatRally is an independent sports analytics research project. It compares pregame probabilities with available Kalshi quotes and tracks hypothetical paper results.

| Market | What the model estimates |
|---|---|
| **NHL Moneyline** | The probability a team wins the full game. |
| **NFL Anytime Touchdown (ATD)** | The probability a player scores at least one touchdown. Passing touchdowns thrown do not count as touchdowns scored by the passer. |

A paper signal requires a verified market match, a usable quote, and positive expected value after fees. NFL ATD also requires verified pregame player eligibility. Real-money orders are disabled.

### What the numbers mean

- **Model probability** estimates an NHL team win or an NFL player scoring at least one touchdown.
- **Kalshi Executable Ask:** The price inferred from the current opposing orderbook bids at the hypothetical contract size. A displayed quote is **not** an actual execution.
- **Fee and Orderbook Depth:** The estimated or verified applicable transaction cost and the available quantity supporting that quote.
- **Net EV:** Hypothetical expected value after applicable fees, shown only when the required market and fee inputs are verified. Otherwise the game stays **NO BET**.
- **Odds Tier:** **Underdog** (selected YES ask below $0.50), **Short Favorite** ($0.50–$0.59), or **Heavy Favorite** ($0.60+).
- **CLV:** The movement from the recorded pregame entry ask to the observed near-puck-drop closing ask; a **quote-to-quote proxy**, not a realized fill.

## Current launch status

- **NHL Moneyline** uses pregame quotes, near-puck-drop closing quotes, and verified settlements to measure hypothetical results.
- **NFL ATD** has scheduled paper evaluation and reporting. Live end-to-end delivery remains unverified. Incomplete player eligibility, market, quote, or fee evidence blocks qualification.

The research aims to collect and review **150–250 completed, independently logged paper games** before evaluating the evidence. That sample target does **not** automatically authorize real trading.

## Other sports and historical research

Earlier NHL Even/Odd publishing was retired in favor of Moneyline research. Historical Even/Odd, Over/Under, and soccer research remain available for reproducibility. La Liga BTTS is deferred.

Configured markets do not guarantee a game, a qualified prediction, or an available contract on every date.

## Transparency

StatRally provides independent research and hypothetical paper-trading analytics, **not financial advice or guarantees**. Probabilities can be inaccurate; market prices change, liquidity may be insufficient, and fees reduce potential returns. Historical backtests and paper results do not establish future profitability.

**No real-money orders are enabled.**
