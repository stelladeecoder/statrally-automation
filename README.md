## What is StatRally?

StatRally tracks **EVEN/ODD total scores** across soccer and hockey. An EVEN result means the combined final score is an even number; an ODD result means it is odd. Competition-specific scoring rules matter, including soccer's 90-minute result and the NHL's final-score treatment.

Our goal is to make it easier to follow upcoming games, explore independently researched probabilities, and evaluate results over time.

**StatRally's current Even/Odd models are experimental.** Their probabilities have not been established as calibrated, profitable, or suitable for betting recommendations.

## The StatRally experience

- **Match watchlists:** Upcoming games and start times, organized by sport.
- **Even/Odd research:** Experimental analysis, separate from the watchlist display.
- **Results:** Independently checked final-score outcomes where available.
- **Performance tracking:** Transparent evaluation of original, timestamped research forecasts.
- **Free access:** Community and web experiences are intended to be free.

Watchlists contain **matchups and scheduled times only**. Model probabilities and market odds belong in dedicated picks experiences **only after the necessary validation and price checks**. No qualified betting picks are currently activated by the parity research beta.

## Transparency

StatRally is an independent research and information project. It does not place trades or wagers. Historical model results do not guarantee future outcomes, and a probability estimate alone does not demonstrate a betting edge.
